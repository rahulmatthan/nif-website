Two programmes, one purpose

NIF Book Fellowships

{{ site.Data.status.bookFellowship.amount }}
Per fellow · twelve months

Awarded annually to scholars and writers working on any aspect of India after 1947. ₹1 lakh a month for twelve months, with the balance of ₹6 lakh on submission of the final manuscript — plus editorial, legal and administrative guidance from proposal to publication, and introductions to publishers and agents.

NIF Book Prize

{{ site.Data.status.bookPrize.amount }}
Annual · single winner

Awarded every year since 2018 for the best non-fiction book on modern or contemporary India published in the preceding year. Open to writers of any nationality, for work written in English or translated into English from any language.
