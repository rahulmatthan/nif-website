Before you apply

What we ask for

A proposal

Two to three thousand words setting out the question the book answers, the sources it will rest on, and why it matters now.

A sample chapter

Or, for translations, ten pages of the translated text alongside the corresponding source pages.

A working schedule

A month-by-month plan showing how the manuscript will be finished inside the term of the fellowship.
