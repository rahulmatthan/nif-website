Eligibility

What can be entered

  • Any nationality.There is no restriction on where the writer is from or lives.
  • Non-fiction only, any genre.History, biography, political commentary, travel, memoir, reportage — anything that offers insight into the making of modern India.
  • English or translated into English.Books originally written in an Indian language and translated into English are eligible, and have won.
  • Professionally published.Books must have national or international distribution. Self-published works are not eligible.
  • Published in the preceding year.The 2027 prize considers books published in 2026.
  • Not an NIF fellowship book.Books supported by a Foundation fellowship are not eligible for the prize.

Questions

Book Prize FAQs

How is a book nominated?
Publishers and literary agents may nominate titles from November. From January, authors may also submit their own books. The Foundation's team and jury additionally consider eligible books on their own initiative, so a book need not be formally nominated to be read. Write to {{ site.Params.contact.submissions }}.
What is the prize money?
The winner receives ₹15,00,000. Where the winning book is a translation, the award is shared between author and translator.
When was the prize established?
In 2018. The first prize was awarded that year to Milan Vaishnav for When Crime Pays.
Are translated works considered?
Yes. Books originally written in Indian languages and translated into English are eligible. Shekhar Pathak's The Chipko Movement, translated from the Hindi by Manisha Chaudhry, won in 2022.
Are self-published books or e-books eligible?
Only professionally published books with national or international distribution are considered. Self-published works are not eligible.
What genres are eligible?
There is no limitation within non-fiction. History, biography, political commentary, travelogue, memoir, reportage and academic monographs have all been longlisted or have won.
Who judges the prize?
The Governing Board of the New India Foundation, chaired for the prize by {{ $jury.chair }}, with external specialist readers brought in where a submission requires them.
Is there a fee to enter?
No. There is no entry fee for nomination by a publisher, an agent or an author.
