What it is

{{- if $isBook }}

The NIF Book Fellowship is awarded annually to scholars and writers working on any aspect of the history of independent India. ₹1,00,000 a month for twelve months, with the balance of ₹6,00,000 on submission of the final manuscript. Beyond the stipend, the Fellowship provides editorial, legal and administrative guidance from proposal to publication, introductions to publishers and agents, and community support. Open to Indian nationals, including those living abroad.

{{- else }}

The NIF Translation Fellowship awards ₹6,00,000 over six months to translate a significant non-fiction work into English from one of ten Indian languages: Assamese, Bangla, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Tamil and Urdu. Applicants should be fluent in the source language and English and have prior translation experience. Open equally to established translators and debutants. Recipients commit full time for the duration; the fellowship is non-renewable.
